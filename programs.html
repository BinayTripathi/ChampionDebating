Questions

Before you enrol.

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp at +61 470 613 864.

Is there a free trial class?

Yes. Book a free trial class and see the training for yourself before you commit.

How much are classes?

$75 + GST per student for each 2-hour session. Fees are paid one term at a time.

What if my child misses a class?

For a planned absence, you receive a credit of one class fee at the start of the term. For an unplanned absence, your child makes it up through a lesson, a video and homework tasks.

How much homework is there?

Each week students receive a matter file of reading and research, adjusted to age and skill: around 400 words for beginners and 500 words for advanced students (negotiable). Feedback and homework are shared through Google Classroom.

When and where are classes held?

Classes run weekly at our Balwyn campus, 255 Whitehorse Road, and at Glen Waverley. Each session is two hours. Times are confirmed at enrolment; see the sample timetable above.

Are classes taught in English?

Yes. All training is delivered in English, preparing students for school debating, university applications and international competition.

Do you offer a referral credit?

Yes. Families who refer a new student receive a $50 credit on their term invoice for every successful referral.
