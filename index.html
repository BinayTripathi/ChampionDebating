Motion of the week

This house would ban homework in primary schools.

Which side would you take?

“Young children have few free hours. Every one spent on worksheets is an hour not spent playing, reading for fun or sleeping, and those build learning too.”

Coach’s tipLead with what children lose, not with what homework fails to give.

“Homework teaches habits that class time can’t: planning, working alone and asking for help. Ban it, and children first meet those skills in high school, when the stakes are higher.”

Coach’s tipDon’t defend all homework. Defend the right kind: short, purposeful and age-appropriate.

Our philosophy

Confidence comes from clear thinking.

Every student learns to think before they speak, and to speak so that others listen.

  1. i.

    Focus on the problem in front of you

    Students learn to stay present, identify the core issue and enter a state of flow rather than being overwhelmed by pressure.

  2. ii.

    Structure, then freedom

    Clear frameworks, concrete plans and disciplined practice, with room for each student’s own style and judgement.

  3. iii.

    English-first, globally minded

    All training is delivered in English, preparing students for school debating, university applications and international competition.
