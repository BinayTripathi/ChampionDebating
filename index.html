Motion of the week

This house would ban homework in primary schools.

Which side would you take?

“Young children have few free hours. Every one spent on worksheets is an hour not spent playing, reading for fun or sleeping, and those build learning too.”

Coach’s tipLead with what children lose, not with what homework fails to give.

“Homework teaches habits that class time can’t: planning, working alone and asking for help. Ban it, and children first meet those skills in high school, when the stakes are higher.”

Coach’s tipDon’t defend all homework. Defend the right kind: short, purposeful and age-appropriate.

Our philosophy

Not tutoring. Coaching, from people who have won.

Champion Debating was founded by Sydney debating coaches who saw gaps in private coaching. We hand-pick our coaches from leading university debating societies, and we hold all of our teaching to three principles.

  1. i.

    Hands-on

    Students practise often and get feedback every time. We focus on using what they learn, so confidence grows in the room and not just in their notes.

  2. ii.

    Inspiring

    Our coaches are experienced and passionate, and they teach current material. They help each student set goals of their own, then chase them.

  3. iii.

    Accountable

    We take responsibility for every student's progress. That means personal support, regular feedback and resources for each child, every term.

The pathway

From first words to the final round.

Every student moves along the same pathway at their own pace: first we make speaking fun, then safe, then sharp. Along the way they build the skills selectors look for in ISDA, Archdale and GPS trials.

I

Build passion

Lessons built around games make debating and speaking something children look forward to.

  • Speaking games
  • Team roles
  • Fun first
II

Inspire confidence

Students speak to larger audiences in small, steady steps, until the nerves settle and the voice carries.

  • Projection
  • Pace & tone
  • No more “um”
III

Master the craft

Rigorous teaching in argument and presentation, with exercises that get harder each week.

  • Rebuttal
  • Case building
  • Thinking on your feet

What they master along the way

Expression

  • Clear articulation
  • Voice projection
  • No filler words
  • Pace, tone & emphasis

Structure

  • Principled & practical arguments
  • Integrated rebuttal
  • Case structure
  • Winning the clash

Knowledge

  • Forms of argument
  • Global affairs
  • Common topic areas
  • Thinking under pressure
