本周辩题

This house would ban homework in primary schools.

你会选择哪一方？

“Young children have few free hours. Every one spent on worksheets is an hour not spent playing, reading for fun or sleeping, and those build learning too.”

教练提示Lead with what children lose, not with what homework fails to give.

“Homework teaches habits that class time can’t: planning, working alone and asking for help. Ban it, and children first meet those skills in high school, when the stakes are higher.”

教练提示Don’t defend all homework. Defend the right kind: short, purposeful and age-appropriate.

我们的理念

自信来自清晰的思考。

每位学生都学会先思考再表达，并让自己的表达值得被倾听。

  1. i.

    专注眼前的问题

    我们教学生专注当下、抓住核心矛盾，进入心流状态，而不是被压力淹没。

  2. ii.

    先有结构，再求灵活

    清晰的框架、具体的计划与扎实的练习，同时给每位学生留出发挥个人风格的空间。

  3. iii.

    以英语为核心，放眼全球

    全部课程以英语授课，帮助学生为校内辩论、大学申请和国际赛事做好准备。
