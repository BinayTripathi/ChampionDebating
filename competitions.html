Term 3 Debate Showcase.

Students debated for their families in September 2026: two debates on one motion each, for primary and secondary groups.

Primary motion

Secondary motion

That teenagers whom the government believes are at risk of committing crimes should be made to do a 6-month bootcamp (which could include fitness courses, community work, behaviour management training and work experience).
