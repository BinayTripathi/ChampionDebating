Every Friday

The schools league.

A weekly debating league held on Zoom every Friday after school. Teams from schools across Sydney face each other through the term, with adjudication and feedback from our coaches.

Enter your school
  1. i.

    Weekly rounds

    A fresh motion every Friday. Students get regular competitive practice without giving up a weekend.

  2. ii.

    Expert adjudication

    Every debate is judged by an experienced adjudicator, and every speaker receives written feedback.

  3. iii.

    Accessible entry

    Entry is $200 per school for the season, so any school can field a team.
